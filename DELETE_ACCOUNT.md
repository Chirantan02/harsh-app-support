# Harsh: Screen Time Blocker — Delete Your Account & Data

**App:** Harsh: Screen Time Blocker (`com.antibrainrot.blocker`)
**Last updated:** September 16, 2026

Harsh has **no login and no user accounts**. There is no sign-up, no email login,
no Google/Apple sign-in — the app works anonymously. Most of your data
(monitored apps, contracts, confessions, session history, lockouts) is stored
**only on your device** and never uploaded anywhere.

## How to request deletion

**Option 1 — Delete on-device data yourself (instant, no email needed):**

1. Open Harsh → Settings → clear or reset any data you want gone, then
2. Uninstall the app (long-press the Harsh icon → Uninstall).

Uninstalling removes 100% of locally stored data immediately. Nothing remains
on any server because nothing was ever uploaded.

**Option 2 — Request deletion of server-side records (email us):**

Some records live with third-party processors (purchases, anonymous analytics).
To request deletion of anything traceable to you:

1. Email **support@antibrainrot.app** with the subject **"Delete my data"**.
2. In the email, include your Google Play order number (format `GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx`,
   found on your Play receipt email or at
   [pay.google.com](https://pay.google.com) → Activity).
3. We confirm deletion by reply email within **30 days**.

## What gets deleted vs. what is kept

| Data | Where it lives | Deleted? | Retention |
|------|---------------|----------|-----------|
| Monitored apps, contracts, confessions, session history, lockouts | Your device only | Yes — instantly on uninstall | 0 days (never uploaded) |
| Support emails (your address + order/ID you send us) | Our inbox | Yes, on request | Deleted within 30 days of your request |
| Purchase entitlement records | RevenueCat (anonymous app-user ID) | On request — we forward erasure to RevenueCat | Per [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| Play billing records (order numbers, receipts) | Google Play | Cannot be deleted by us — managed by Google | Per [Google's retention policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Anonymous analytics events (screens viewed, onboarding progress) | Firebase Analytics | Not traceable to you — cannot be linked or individually deleted | Aggregated; auto-expire per Firebase defaults |

We never see your payment details (handled entirely by Google Play Billing),
and we never sell or share personal data with advertisers.
