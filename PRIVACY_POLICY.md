# Harsh: Screen Time Control — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Harsh ("the app") is built to be local-first. Your app usage monitoring, session history, contracts, confessions, and lockout data are processed and stored **on your device only**. We do not collect, transmit, or sell this data.

## What we collect

1. **Identifiers — linked to you.** If you sign in with Google, Google provides an account identifier and email. This is used to create and identify your account and to sync your purchase entitlements. It is never shared with third parties for advertising.

2. **Usage data — not linked to you.** We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. screens viewed, onboarding progress). These are not linked to your identity.

3. **Purchase history — not linked to you.** Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat under an anonymous app-user ID. We never see your payment details.

## What we do NOT collect

- The apps you monitor, when you open them, or how long you use them (this stays on-device).
- The text you write in contracts, confessions, or bypasses (stored locally, never uploaded).
- Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.

## Permissions used

- **Usage access / Accessibility** — to detect when you open an app you chose to monitor, so the app can intervene. This data never leaves your device.
- **Display over other apps** — to show the contract, guillotine, and lockout screens on top of monitored apps.
- **Notifications** — to deliver your scheduled reminders and intervention prompts.

## Third-party services

- [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) — authentication and analytics.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) — purchase entitlement management.

## Children

Harsh is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your choices

### Requesting deletion of your data

To request deletion of the data associated with your account (your Google-account identifier, email, and purchase entitlement records), email **support@antibrainrot.app** with the subject "Delete my data" from the address you use in the app. We delete the account identifier and email within 30 days. Purchase records retained by Google Play and RevenueCat are governed by their retention policies; anonymized analytics aggregates cannot be traced back to you. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately.
